# Launcher

**Planned: mock-ups come before code.**

The home screen, built as a module on top of the GrapheneOS Launcher3 sources. Gesture animations and the recents screen stay stock.

Mock-ups of the launcher are not drawn yet.

## Features

- Our own design for the home screen and dock, the app drawer and search, folders, popup menus and the widget picker.
- Settings: grids, icon and label size, hidden apps, icon packs with masks, calendar icons and per-app icons.
- Gestures: double tap to lock, swipe down for notifications or quick settings.
- Search finds apps only.

## Permissions and network

- No network access.
- No new privileged permissions.
- Hidden apps are hidden from the drawer only, not protected. To protect apps, use Private Space.
- Updated with the system, not through the store.
