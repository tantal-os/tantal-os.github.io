# Phone

**Designed: work on the app has not started.**

Calls and contacts in one app: contacts are a tab, not a separate app. It works over the system Telecom stack and the contacts provider, so contacts synced by other apps show up.

## Features

- Recents, contacts and keypad tabs.
- Contact card and editing.
- Incoming call and in-call screens.
- Contact shortcuts on the home screen.
- The current call in the dynamic island.

## Permissions and network

- The default phone app role, contacts and call log.
- No network access, no privileged permissions.
