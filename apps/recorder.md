## Features

- List of recordings.
- Recording with a live waveform and markers.
- Playback with 10-second skips.
- The current recording in the dynamic island.

## Planned

- Transcription on the device, through the AI core.

## Permissions and network

- Microphone and notifications.
- No network access.
