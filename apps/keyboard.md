## Features

- English and Russian layouts.
- Suggestions, symbols and emoji.
- A paste button above the keys.
- Settings: languages, auto-correction, key height, vibration, a personal dictionary.

## Planned

- Our own engine: predictive input and swipe typing.
- Voice input through the on-device AI core.

## Permissions and network

- No network access.
