# Calendar

**In development** · Source: [tantal-os/calendar](https://github.com/tantal-os/calendar)

A calendar over the system calendar provider. Calendars synced by other apps show up and stay in sync; without any account the app keeps a calendar on the device.

## Features

- Month, week, day and schedule views.
- Search.
- Creating and editing events: repeats, reminders, a choice of calendar.
- Reminder notifications.

## Planned

- A home screen widget.
- Repeat rules beyond a plain frequency.
- Guests.

## Permissions and network

- Calendar, read and write: events live in the system calendar provider.
- Notifications: reminders.
- No network access.
