## Features

- Alarms with rising volume, snooze and a ring screen.
- Alarms ring even before the first unlock after a reboot.
- World clock.
- Several timers at once.
- Stopwatch with laps.

## Planned

- A home screen widget.
- Live Updates for running timers, shown in the dynamic island.

## Permissions and network

- Exact alarms: alarms ring on time.
- Full-screen notifications: the ring screen over the lock screen.
- A foreground service: a ringing alarm or timer keeps running.
- Start at boot: alarms come back after a reboot.
- Notifications, vibration, keeping the screen awake while ringing.
- No network access.
